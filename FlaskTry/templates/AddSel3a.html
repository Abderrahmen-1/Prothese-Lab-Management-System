Khalsa vs Versement

Khalsa: Complete payment - The full amount has been paid.

Versement: Partial payment - Only a portion has been paid, enter the advance amount.

Tips for Adding Items

  • Use clear, descriptive names for items
  • Include the exact purchase price
  • Set the correct purchase date
  • Specify payment status accurately
  • Include versement amount if applicable

Required Fields

  • Item Name - The name of the material or component
  • Price - The total cost of the item
  • Purchase Date - When the item was purchased
  • Payment Status - Whether fully paid (Khalsa) or partial (Versement)
